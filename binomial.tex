P(0 \text{ wins (X=0)}) = \binom{100}{0} \cdot 0.01^{0} \cdot (1 - 0.01)^{100}
\]
\[
= 1 \cdot 1 \cdot 0.99^{100}
\]
\[
P(X=0)= 0.366
\]
\[
P(\text{at least }1 \text{ win (}X \ge 1\text{)}) = 1 - P(X = 0)
\]
\[
= 1 - 0.366
\]
\[
P(X \ge 1 ) \approx 0.634
